Delivery workflow

Use project boards to move tasks through Backlog, To Do, In Progress, In Review, Done and Cancelled. Open Sprints for timeboxed delivery, Modules for workstreams, and Pages for project knowledge.

Each project has 50 dated tasks and five sprints with 10 tasks each. Every sprint includes all workflow statuses.

Personal stickies can be edited by their owner. Workspace admins manage workspace settings; members participate in project delivery.
